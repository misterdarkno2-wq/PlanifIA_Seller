PlanifIA Privacy Policy

Version of October 22, 2026 (current). It applies to the website planifia.cl and to the PlanifIA app for Android (cl.planifia.app). Previous versions are in the version history.

In short

  • We use your data to provide the service: your account, your goals, the AI that proposes plans and the chat with Lumi.
  • You can sign in with your email and a password, or with your Google account. With Google we only receive your name, your email, your profile photo and your Google account identifier.
  • We don't sell your data, we don't use our own analytics and we don't train models with what you write.
  • The AI runs on our own server in Chile; if it isn't available, Groq's backup AI answers, in the United States.
  • The Free plan of the Android app shows Google AdMob ads. They are only personalized if you give your consent where the law requires it; in Chile they are always non-personalized.
  • In the Android app you can turn on a daily reminder. Your phone schedules the notification and it never shows the names of your goals.
  • You can view, correct, export and delete your data from Settings, and exercise all your rights by writing to us.

What we don't do: we don't sell your data, we don't share it for marketing purposes, we don't use our own analytics tools and we don't use what you write to train AI models. The notification says how many steps you have that day, without the names of your goals, actions or habits, because it is visible with the phone locked. Sensitive data: you don't need to provide health data or other sensitive data to use PlanifIA. If you write it in your goals or in the chat, we process it only to provide the service, like the rest of your content. 3. Artificial intelligence and automated decisions

Plans and Lumi's replies are automatic suggestions: you decide what to save. The AI doesn't make decisions that produce legal effects on you or significantly affect you. Some measures are automatic: the AI and ad usage limits, the crisis review described above and the detection of credit abuse. You can ask for a person to review any of those measures by writing to us.

Each Lumi reply and each plan has a Report button. We review reports and may adjust the AI's instructions.

4. Ads in the Android app

The Free plan of the Android app shows Google AdMob ads: a video when you use the AI (at most one every 5 minutes) and optional ads to earn credits. The Plus and Pro plans, the Remove ads purchase (except optional ads) and the website show no ads.

When the law requires it (for example, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland), the app asks for your consent with Google's tool before loading any ad, and you can change it in Settings → Ads and privacy. Ads are only personalized (chosen according to your interests) if you give that consent. In other cases, including Chile, where it isn't requested, the app asks Google for non-personalized ads: they are chosen based on the app's content and your approximate location (for example, the city), not on your past behavior. Google still uses the advertising identifier to limit how many times you see an ad and to measure ads in aggregate. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in Android settings (Privacy → Ads). More information on how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

8. How we protect your data

  • All communication uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Each account can only read its own data, thanks to access rules in the database; changes go through server functions that validate each piece of data.
  • Service keys (AI, payments and ads) only exist on the server, never in the app or on the website.
  • The website limits the resources it can load (content security policy) and the app doesn't allow unencrypted connections.
  • The AI server only accepts authenticated connections through an encrypted tunnel and doesn't log the content of messages.
  • We limit AI usage per account and per connection to prevent abuse.

No system is infallible. If a security breach affecting your data occurs, we will report it without undue delay to Chile's Personal Data Protection Agency and, when the law requires it or it may affect you, to you as well, with what happened and what you can do.

9. Your rights and how to exercise them

You have the right to:

  • Access: know what data of yours we process, why, where it comes from and to whom it is disclosed.
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: ask us to delete your data.
  • Objection: object to a processing, for example one based on legitimate interest.
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured and commonly used format.
  • Blocking: ask us to temporarily suspend processing while another request of yours is resolved.
  • Not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing, and to ask for a person to review an automatic measure.

From the app, instantly: correct your profile in Settings, download your data in Settings → Export my data (a JSON file with your goals, chat, AI requests, credits, purchases and acceptances) and delete your account in Settings → Delete account or by following these instructions.

By email: write to planifiaprivacity@gmail.com from your account's email, saying which right you want to exercise. It's free. We will reply within 15 calendar days. If you ask for the temporary blocking of your data while we resolve another request of yours, we apply it within 2 business days.

If we don't reply in time or you disagree with the answer, you can file a complaint with Chile's Personal Data Protection Agency (Agencia de Protección de Datos Personales), through the channels it publishes when it begins operating, or before the courts.

10. Minors

PlanifIA is intended for people aged 18 or older: when creating the account, or the first time you sign in with Google, you confirm your age. It is not designed for children or teenagers. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete it. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child created an account, write to us.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make an important change we will notify you in the app before it takes effect and ask you to accept it to keep using the AI and buying. If you don't agree, you can export your data and delete your account. Each version stays published with its date in the history.
