Delete your PlanifIA account

You can delete your account and all its data at any time, from the app or the website, without contacting us.

From the app or the website

  1. Open the PlanifIA app or go to planifia.cl and sign in (with your email or with Continue with Google).
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Under Delete account, tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE (or ELIMINAR if the app is in Spanish) and confirm.

If you can't sign in

Write to planifiaprivacity@gmail.com from your account's email with the subject “Delete my account”. We will delete it within 7 days at most.

What is deleted

The following are deleted immediately and permanently from our database: your account and email, the data Google provided if you signed in with Google (name, profile photo and identifier), your profile and availability, goals, milestones, actions, habits, Lumi's progress, your conversation with Lumi, AI requests and usage, credits and invitations, purchase and subscription records, imports, acceptances of the terms and content reports.

Your credits are lost and can't be recovered. If you have a Google Play subscription, also cancel it in Google Play so it doesn't renew. If you signed in with Google, you can also remove PlanifIA's access in your Google Account → Connections to third-party apps & services.

What may remain for a while

  • The database provider's technical logs, which are deleted automatically within the periods set out in the privacy policy. We don't use them for anything else.
  • Google keeps its own records of purchases made with Google Play and of ads, according to its policies.
  • Emails you have sent us, for the period stated in the privacy policy.

Before deleting your account you can download your data in Settings → Export my data.
